# Corredor Invertido

Browser endless runner (pseudo-3D tunnel), shipped as a PWA on GitHub Pages from `main` (root). No build step, no dependencies: static files and native ES modules.

## Conventions

- Keep `js/sim.js` free of drawing and audio. It reports events through `emit(type, data)`; `main.js` routes them to `audio.js`. The renderer (`js/render.js`) only reads state.
- `js/lyre.js` is shared with the `guilhermrosa/lira-de-bolso` repository. Keep both copies identical; change one, copy to the other.
- When adding or renaming a file the game loads, add it to `SHELL` in `sw.js`.
- On every release, bump `APP_VERSION` in `js/version.js`, the only copy of the version: the menu label and the service worker cache name (`sw.js` imports it) both read it.
- UI text is in Brazilian Portuguese.
- Test with a local server (`python3 -m http.server`); modules do not load from `file://`.
- Automated tests (v0.35): `npm test` (or `node --test`), Node 22+, no dependencies; `package.json` exists only for this. `tests/solver.js` is a brute-force player over the real sim (nothing / tap / swipe down every 50 ms, strict: slipping into a hole counts as failing). Tests prove every authored stretch at 12/16/21 m/s from both surfaces, random generator windows from 100 to 2000 m, every tutorial step, and the rules (hole grace, lives, golden heart). Run them after any change to sim.js, config.js, sequences.js or tutorial.js; a new obstacle or stretch must keep them green.
- Code layout (v0.35): `main.js` = loop, game flow, menus and keys; `options.js` = Opções state, labels and buttons; `input.js` = touch gestures; `panels.js` = stats panel, achievements list and toast; `store.js` = localStorage wrapper; `sequences.js` = authored stretches (data).

## Story and the divine

- The world is ruins of ancient Greece. The gods bless the axolotl from time to time, helping him find something or reach somewhere (the story is still open). The inverted gravity is itself such a blessing: the gravity is his, not the world's.
- Gold is the sign of anything divine. v0.30: while inverted, his gills turn gold with a soft halo behind the head, gold sparks burst on every flip, and gold motes drift off him toward the roof; falling through the roof reads "Caiu no céu". Future boosters are meant to be gifts of the gods (Hermes' sandals, something of Zeus…) and should reuse this gold language.
- Open ideas (not decided, recorded in the project plan doc): Zeus is against him (toppling columns and dropping blocks as his wrath, electric white-blue as his colour, opposite the allies' gold); boosters from Hermes (winged sandals: speed or double jump), Athena (aegis: absorbs one hit), Apollo (golden lyre: short slow motion), Poseidon (rescue from a fall), Hera or Iris (rainbow: shows obstacles ahead). Story told only through a line in the menu or at the end of a run, and the god's name when a booster is taken.
- Future stretch / challenge idea (user, v0.35, not now): a stretch with neither floor nor roof, where the hero must keep flipping in mid-air to stay between them until a surface returns (falling out of either side ends it). Notes for then: today flips only work from a jump or the hole grace, so it needs a free mid-air flip (with a cooldown or a limit so it stays a skill); the v0.33 grace already lets players juggle flip/slip across two voids, which this stretch would replace with a deliberate rule; the solver tests would need it too.

## Design state

- Chosen sound: lyre preset "Ruína" (chip 24, bright 30, sustain 80, echo 37), Dorian mode on D. Effects and music react to the game.
- Main character placeholder: red humanoid axolotl drawn from behind after the user's silhouette sketch (big oval head, three long fringed gills per side, slim body, asymmetric running pose with a raised open hand and a kicked-back leg showing its sole, long tail with a crest) in `js/hero.js`; the golem of the ruin is the runner-up. World placeholder (v0.8): the tunnel is a seaside stoa of white marble on a sunny day. Floor = marble lane, ceiling = coffered roof with a painted frieze, sides = columns with sky and sea between. Missing surfaces are simply not drawn so the backdrop shows (open sky above, sea below); columns break where the roof is gone. Obstacles: low = low wall or fallen column drum, tall floor = ruined wall, tall ceiling = fallen painted lintel. Palette lives in `COL` (`js/config.js`). The user's own visual prototypes will replace this.
- Camera: chase view turned about 8° to the left (`CAMERA` in `js/render.js`). The stronger three-quarter view from the user's framing sketch (v0.12–0.13) was judged too sideways; its values are noted in the comment. The camera mirrors while rolling onto the ceiling so the screen composition stays the same.
- Slide (v0.13): swipe down / ↓ / S. On a surface it slides for `SLIDE_TIME` with height `SLIDE_H`; in the air it drops fast and slides on landing. Every touch acts at once (v0.19: the 110 ms air wait was removed after feedback that flips felt slow); a drag after an air tap flips and then drops fast onto the new surface, sliding on landing. Flips keep momentum toward the new surface with a minimum of `FLIP_V`. Swipe up (v0.28, after playtesters kept trying it) flips too: dragging away from the hero's surface flips, toward it slides; from a surface that is the touch's jump plus a flip in one gesture, and a touch that already flipped is not flipped back. With the `fixed` camera on the roof the screen directions are reversed so the rule follows the hero. `press()` returns 'jump' | 'flip' | 'buffer'. Fallen portals (`type: 'portal'`, from `PORTAL_FROM` m) are solid from just above a `PORTAL_GAP` gap on their side to the opposite surface, so the only way past is sliding on that side.
- Layout: everything lives in `#stage`, a portrait frame capped at 9:16 and centred, with solid `--side` bars on wide screens (computers); on phones it fills the screen. HUD and overlays are positioned inside the stage. v0.35.1: its height is held in `--app-h` by `lockStage()` (main.js) through shrinks under 20% with the same width, so Android's gesture navigation bar sliding in covers the bottom edge instead of resizing the game (user found the resize annoying); a rotation, a taller screen or a bigger change resets it. v0.35.2: the bars appear as translucent overlays yet the HUD and menus still jumped, because Android reports larger safe-area insets while they show; `lockStage()` now reads the insets from `#safeProbe` and holds their smallest value in `--safe-*` (CSS uses those instead of `env()` directly). v0.36: switched to `display: standalone` at the user's request (in fullscreen, after pulling either system bar Android reserved the status bar strip in black and moved the whole window down, which the page cannot prevent). The status bar now always shows, in `theme_color` #deecf2. Android applies a display change after reinstalling the app (or when Chrome refreshes the installed app on its own). The user reinstalled and the data survived: an installed PWA keeps its localStorage in Chrome's storage for the origin, so uninstalling does not wipe it (the v0.36 backup feature was removed in v0.36.1 as unneeded). v0.36.2: back to `display: fullscreen` at the user's request (standalone also kept the bottom navigation bar always shown; the user prefers more screen and accepts the jump for now). Plan: as a store app, a native wrapper (Capacitor, the user's choice) for true fullscreen with transient bars; a Trusted Web Activity needs Digital Asset Links at the origin root (`/.well-known/assetlinks.json`), which a github.io project page can't serve (needs a `guilhermrosa.github.io` user-site repo or a custom domain).
- Options (menu → "Opções"): music, effects, vibration (`ci-vibrate`, off by default; the user dislikes it), mirror (`ci-mirror-v2`, ON by default since v0.29 so the corridor runs off to the left, clear of a right thumb; flips the canvas with CSS; HUD and menus are not mirrored), camera turn (`ci-camera`: `animated` roll, `instant` cut with a light fade, or `fixed`: no roll at all, camera at mid-height, the hero runs upside down along the roof; defaults to instant under prefers-reduced-motion), swipe sensitivity (`ci-swipe`: 40 / 26 / 14 px).
- Game feel (`js/fx.js`, cosmetic only): dust on landing (sim emits `land`) and while sliding, a 90 ms hit-stop plus stone chips on a hit, speed lines from 13 m/s, a "N m" pop at each 100 m. Particles are in world coordinates and drawn by the renderer from `opts.fx`. Game over shows sooner (380 / 600 ms) and accepts a restart tap after 450 ms.
- Tutorial (`js/tutorial.js`): runs on the first "Jogar" (until completed or skipped; flag `ci-tutorial-done`) and from the menu's "Tutorial" button. Five steps (low wall, hole, flip, flip back, portal), each one obstacle on the player's current surface in a scripted sim (`createSim({ scripted: true })`); dying repeats only that step.
- Pause: HUD button, Esc or P, and automatically when the app leaves the screen; resuming runs a 3-2-1 countdown.
- Stats (`js/stats.js`, localStorage `ci-stats`, never sent anywhere): runs, distance, time played, deaths by obstacle kind (`obstacleKind()` in sim.js), distance ranges, actions, tutorial retries per step, last 20 runs. Menu → "Estatísticas" shows them, with "Copiar dados" (JSON) and a two-tap reset.
- Lives (v0.23): `LIVES` = 2 per run, shown as placeholder hearts under the distance. A hit marks that obstacle `spent`; a fall is rescued by clearing obstacles overlapping the next 14 m. Either way `INVULN_TIME` of protection follows (hero blinks) and the sim emits `hurt`; the last life ends the run as before. Scripted (tutorial) sims get 1 life so dying repeats the step. Stats keep lives lost by obstacle in `hurts`.
- Golden heart (v0.32; the v0.31 400 m regeneration was removed at the user's request): `type: 'heart'`, Athena's blessing, an extra life on top of the 2 red ones (`S.gold`), at most one held. It spawns from `HEART_FROM` m in the free stretch after a regular obstacle (`HEART_CHANCE`, about one every 600 m), on either surface (`HEART_LIFT` above it), only while none is held or already on its way. Taken by touch even while protected; a hit takes the golden heart first. The sim emits `heal`; main shows "Bênção de Atena: +1 vida", gold sparks and a gold heart in the HUD; stats count it in `heals.athena`.
- Slipping into a hole (v0.33, user request): standing over a hole no longer kills at once. The hero slips in (sim emits `slip`, a short falling sound), sinks slowly to at most `SINK_DEPTH` (always on screen), and a tap or swipe up within `SINK_TIME` (0.25 s) flips him out. The run keeps its speed (v0.34: the v0.33 slowdown to 35% felt like slow motion; `SINK_SLOW` = 1), and the slab ahead keeps crumbling (the hole stretches) so he never sinks into solid floor and the grace is always the full 0.25 s. Otherwise the fall counts as before (life lost and back on the surface, or death). A buffered tap on landing still jumps the hole. The renderer hides his legs only behind the slab in front of the hole.
- Performance (v0.24): `QUALITY` levels in render.js (high / medium / low: canvas resolution cap, draw distance, sea glints, coffers, flutes, speed lines). Opções → "Qualidade" is automatic by default: main.js watches frame times and steps down one level after ~2 s under ~40 fps (`ci-quality-auto`), never back up on its own. Measured in headless Chromium: 60 fps normally; with 6x CPU throttling ~11 fps at high, ~36 fps after stepping down to low.
- Achievements (v0.25, kept basic on purpose): 8 goals in `js/achievements.js` (tutorial, 100/500/1000 m, 300 m without losing a life, 20 flips or 10 slides in a run, 25 runs), stored in `ci-achievements`, checked every 0.5 s during runs and at the end; a toast announces each unlock; menu → "Conquistas" lists them. No daily challenge or sharing yet.
- Falling obstacles (v0.26, from `FALL_FROM` = 200 m): a column of the left colonnade that topples across the path (`topple`, a low block once down: jump it or flip away; it rotates whole around its base corner, no fracture, so the final art can swap it easily) and a roof block that drops to the floor (`drop`, forces a flip up). They start `FALL_LEAD` s before the player arrives (wobble, then an eased fall; sim emits `crack` and `thud`). A toppling column only collides once nearly down; a dropping block collides all the way. The toppling column replaces the decorative left column near it.
- Proportions and speed (v0.29): corridor `HM` 3.6 m (was 3.0) with the camera at 2.45 m, tall blocks 1.55 m, low blocks 0.6 m, jump 1.05 m. Speed eases in: `SPEED_START` 11 → `SPEED_MAX` 21 with `SPEED_EASE` 450 m (about 13 m/s at 100 m, 16 at 300, 18.5 at 600, 20 at 1000), replacing the old linear-in-distance ramp that felt slow then suddenly fast. Icons (tools/make_icons.py) show the seaside colonnade and the axolotl.
- Collisions: `blockHitbox()` / `holeKillZone()` in `js/sim.js` follow each obstacle's drawn shape, trimmed by `HIT` (`js/config.js`) so grazing feels fair; keep them in sync when an obstacle's look changes. Hitbox view: H key, or tap the menu's version label 5 times.
- Authored stretches (v0.35, `js/sequences.js`): set pieces mixed with the generated obstacles (the user wanted both). Timed in seconds of running so they keep their rhythm at any speed. Seven: zigue-zague (tall walls taking turns), muro-e-mureta, escadaria (a long wall on one side, jumps on the other), ponte and ponte-dupla (the user's idea: one surface open with a platform, and a wall on the other side over it forces a flip there and a flip back; "not always", so low weight), portais (slide, flip, slide), ira-dupla (dropping block, then a lintel on the roof). Chance per spawn `sequenceChance()`: 0 before 120 m, 10% rising to 30% at about 2 km.
- Balance notes: low blocks got a larger trim (`HIT.low`) because they were less forgiving than holes; platforms on the open side had little use until the ponte stretches (v0.35).
