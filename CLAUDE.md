# Corredor Invertido

Browser endless runner (pseudo-3D tunnel), shipped as a PWA on GitHub Pages from `main` (root). No build step, no dependencies: static files and native ES modules.

## Conventions

- Keep `js/sim.js` free of drawing and audio. It reports events through `emit(type, data)`; `main.js` routes them to `audio.js`. The renderer (`js/render.js`) only reads state.
- `js/lyre.js` is shared with the `guilhermrosa/lira-de-bolso` repository. Keep both copies identical; change one, copy to the other.
- When adding or renaming a file the game loads, add it to `SHELL` in `sw.js`.
- On every release, bump `APP_VERSION` in `js/version.js`, the only copy of the version: the menu label and the service worker cache name (`sw.js` imports it) both read it.
- Privacy (v0.38 / v0.6.2, user request): nothing leaves the device, so no consent screen is needed. Fonts are served from `fonts/` (latin subset woff2, OFL licences alongside) instead of Google Fonts, so the app contacts no other site (the service worker only handles same-origin requests). `privacidade.html` explains this in plain pt-BR (what stays on the device, how to delete it, hosting by GitHub Pages, no permissions, and the optional feedback form); it is linked from the menu and is the privacy policy URL a store listing will need. Keep it true when adding features that store or send anything (e.g. the planned microphone/audio-file feature of the Lira).
- Commits (user request, 2026-11-13): author and committer are the user, `guilhermrosa <57267848+guilhermrosa@users.noreply.github.com>` (GitHub's no-reply address, so the personal e-mail stays private), set in this repository's `.git/config`; no `Co-Authored-By` or `Claude-Session` lines in commit messages. The whole history was rewritten that way, dates and contents unchanged. In a fresh clone, set `git config user.name` / `user.email` to those values before committing.
- Shared storage (learned 2026-11-13): every GitHub Pages project of the user lives on the same origin, `guilhermrosa.github.io`, so the Corredor and the Lira share one localStorage. The user cleared that site's data in Chrome and lost everything in both apps (game stats/record, and likely the Lira's saved recordings). Decision: no backup or own domain for now; it is solved when the apps become store apps with Capacitor (each app gets its own storage). Until then, warn before telling the user to clear site data.
- UI text is in Brazilian Portuguese.
- Test with a local server (`python3 -m http.server`); modules do not load from `file://`.
- Automated tests (v0.35): `npm test` (or `node --test`), Node 22+, no dependencies; `package.json` exists only for this. `tests/solver.js` is a brute-force player over the real sim (nothing / tap / swipe down every 50 ms, strict: slipping into a hole counts as failing). Tests prove every authored stretch at 12/16/21 m/s from both surfaces, random generator windows from 100 to 2000 m, every tutorial step, and the rules (hole grace, lives, golden heart). Run them after any change to sim.js, config.js, sequences.js or tutorial.js; a new obstacle or stretch must keep them green.
- Visual history (`docs/evolucao/`, user request): a gallery page (served by Pages at /docs/evolucao/) with menu / floor / roof screenshots per milestone version, from the 0.5 greybox prototype (taken from the old Corredor artifact) on. On every release with a visible change, run `node tools/capture-versions.cjs <version>=HEAD` (Playwright; fixed random seed, same inputs for every version) and add an entry (version, date, pt-BR title and text) to `docs/evolucao/versions.json`. Not part of the game: keep these files out of `SHELL`.
- Art direction test (`prototipos/estilos/`, not part of the game, keep out of `SHELL`): the real sim and touch controls drawn with neutral shapes in the two candidate looks the user is weighing, since all current art is placeholder: A `?estilo=retro` simple 3D polygons at ~146×260 px, Bayer dithering to a 10-colour palette plus grain (rolling camera); B `?estilo=papel` 2.5D paper cut-outs (cards with a white cut edge and a drop shadow that fold up from their surface as they approach, a paper-fibre texture multiplied over everything, fixed camera, the hero turning over like a card on a flip). Which way fits depends on how the user will make the final art (2D drawings → B; 3D models → A; pixel art → either). User verdict: the current placeholder look is better than both test renders; the exploration is parked for later. If the final look is 3D, the user cannot model: Claude builds the models in Blender on the user's computer (Blender Python scripts, run there via a connection to their machine). If it is paper-like, the user draws most of the assets.
- Code layout (v0.35): `main.js` = loop, game flow, menus and keys; `options.js` = Opções state, labels and buttons; `input.js` = touch gestures; `panels.js` = stats panel, achievements list and toast; `store.js` = localStorage wrapper; `sequences.js` = authored stretches (data).

## Story and the divine

- The world is ruins of ancient Greece. The gods bless the axolotl from time to time, helping him find something or reach somewhere (the story is still open). The inverted gravity is itself such a blessing: the gravity is his, not the world's.
- Gold is the sign of anything divine. v0.30: while inverted, his gills turn gold with a soft halo behind the head, gold sparks burst on every flip, and gold motes drift off him toward the roof; falling through the roof reads "Caiu no céu". Future boosters are meant to be gifts of the gods (Hermes' sandals, something of Zeus…) and should reuse this gold language.
- Open ideas (not decided, recorded in the project plan doc): Zeus is against him (toppling columns and dropping blocks as his wrath, electric white-blue as his colour, opposite the allies' gold); boosters from Hermes (winged sandals: speed or double jump), Athena (aegis: absorbs one hit), Apollo (golden lyre: short slow motion), Poseidon (rescue from a fall), Hera or Iris (rainbow: shows obstacles ahead). Story told only through a line in the menu or at the end of a run, and the god's name when a booster is taken.
- Future stretch / challenge idea (user, v0.35, not now): a stretch with neither floor nor roof, where the hero must keep flipping in mid-air to stay between them until a surface returns (falling out of either side ends it). Notes for then: today flips only work from a jump or the hole grace, so it needs a free mid-air flip (with a cooldown or a limit so it stays a skill); the v0.33 grace already lets players juggle flip/slip across two voids, which this stretch would replace with a deliberate rule; the solver tests would need it too.

- Main character placeholder: red humanoid axolotl drawn from behind after the user's silhouette sketch (big oval head, three long fringed gills per side, slim body, asymmetric running pose with a raised open hand and a kicked-back leg showing its sole, long tail with a crest) in `js/hero.js`; the golem of the ruin is the runner-up. v0.45 (user: "less like a skeleton", proportions as in the sketch, legs too long): plump pear-shaped body with broad hips and a marked spine and hip line, short thick arms and legs (hip at −0.24, kicked foot at +0.04, limb widths 0.1–0.14), big wide head (0.25 × 0.17 at −0.75), thick tail drawn behind the body; the slide pose got the same proportions. v0.45.1 (user: "he's chubby"): slimmer: torso half-width 0.10 at the neck to 0.18 at the hips, hips at ±0.08, limb widths 0.08–0.11, thinner tail; legs stay short and the head stays big. World placeholder (v0.8): the tunnel is a seaside stoa of white marble on a sunny day. Floor = marble lane, ceiling = coffered roof with a painted frieze, sides = columns with sky and sea between. Missing surfaces are simply not drawn so the backdrop shows (open sky above, sea below); columns break where the roof is gone. Obstacles: low = low wall or fallen column drum, tall floor = ruined wall, tall ceiling = fallen painted lintel. Palette lives in `COL` (`js/config.js`). - Camera: chase view turned about 8° to the left (`CAMERA` in `js/render.js`). The stronger three-quarter view from the user's framing sketch (v0.12–0.13) was judged too sideways; its values are noted in the comment. - Controls (v0.40, user request: "one gesture per action"; the double tap made a touch in the air flip at once, so there was no way to slide back down during a jump): tap = jump (in the air it is buffered and jumps on landing; while slipping into a hole it flips him out); swipe away from the surface = flip (`flipUp()` in sim.js; from a surface it is a jump plus a flip; once per time in the air, tracked by `S.canFlip`, so jump → swipe down → swipe up works but cannot chain); swipe toward it = slide / fast drop. Keys: Space jumps and, pressed again in the air, flips (v0.40.1, user: a keyboard has no swipe, so the double press stays there); ↑/W flip; ↓/S slide. The second-tap flip is gone; the paragraph below describes the older scheme for history. Tutorial steps 3–4 now say "Arraste para cima".
- Slide (v0.13): swipe down / ↓ / S. On a surface it slides for `SLIDE_TIME` with height `SLIDE_H`; in the air it drops fast and slides on landing. Every touch acts at once (v0.19: the 110 ms air wait was removed after feedback that flips felt slow); a drag after an air tap flips and then drops fast onto the new surface, sliding on landing. Flips keep momentum toward the new surface with a minimum of `FLIP_V`. Swipe up (v0.28, after playtesters kept trying it) flips too: dragging away from the hero's surface flips, toward it slides; from a surface that is the touch's jump plus a flip in one gesture, and a touch that already flipped is not flipped back. With the `fixed` camera on the roof the screen directions are reversed so the rule follows the hero. `press()` returns 'jump' | 'flip' | 'buffer'. Fallen portals (`type: 'portal'`, from `PORTAL_FROM` m) are solid from just above a `PORTAL_GAP` gap on their side to the opposite surface, so the only way past is sliding on that side.
- Layout: everything lives in `#stage`, a portrait frame capped at 9:16 and centred, with solid `--side` bars on wide screens (computers); on phones it fills the screen. HUD and overlays are positioned inside the stage. v0.35.1: its height is held in `--app-h` by `lockStage()` (main.js) through shrinks under 20% with the same width, so Android's gesture navigation bar sliding in covers the bottom edge instead of resizing the game (user found the resize annoying); a rotation, a taller screen or a bigger change resets it. v0.35.2: the bars appear as translucent overlays yet the HUD and menus still jumped, because Android reports larger safe-area insets while they show; `lockStage()` now reads the insets from `#safeProbe` and holds their smallest value in `--safe-*` (CSS uses those instead of `env()` directly). v0.36: switched to `display: standalone` at the user's request (in fullscreen, after pulling either system bar Android reserved the status bar strip in black and moved the whole window down, which the page cannot prevent). The status bar now always shows, in `theme_color` #deecf2. Android applies a display change after reinstalling the app (or when Chrome refreshes the installed app on its own). The user reinstalled and the data survived: an installed PWA keeps its localStorage in Chrome's storage for the origin, so uninstalling does not wipe it (the v0.36 backup feature was removed in v0.36.1 as unneeded). v0.36.2: back to `display: fullscreen` at the user's request (standalone also kept the bottom navigation bar always shown; the user prefers more screen and accepts the jump for now). - Playtest feedback form (v0.36.2): `tools/feedback-form.gs`, a Google Apps Script the user runs once at script.google.com to create the Google Form (profile, controls and mechanics grid, accidental flips, camera, hardest obstacles, experience grid, 1–10 score, free text, game version, pasted stats JSON) with a response spreadsheet. - Lives (v0.23): `LIVES` = 2 per run, shown as placeholder hearts under the distance. A hit marks that obstacle `spent`; a fall is rescued by clearing obstacles overlapping the next 14 m. Either way `INVULN_TIME` of protection follows (hero blinks) and the sim emits `hurt`; the last life ends the run as before. Scripted (tutorial) sims get 1 life so dying repeats the step. Stats keep lives lost by obstacle in `hurts`.
- Golden heart (v0.32; the v0.31 400 m regeneration was removed at the user's request): `type: 'heart'`, Athena's blessing, an extra life on top of the 2 red ones (`S.gold`), at most one held. It spawns from `HEART_FROM` m in the free stretch after a regular obstacle (`HEART_CHANCE`, about one every 600 m), on either surface (`HEART_LIFT` above it), only while none is held or already on its way. Taken by touch even while protected; a hit takes the golden heart first. The sim emits `heal`; main shows "Bênção de Atena: +1 vida", gold sparks and a gold heart in the HUD; stats count it in `heals.athena`.
- Slipping into a hole (v0.33, user request): standing over a hole no longer kills at once. The hero slips in (sim emits `slip`, a short falling sound), sinks slowly to at most `SINK_DEPTH` (always on screen), and a tap or swipe up within `SINK_TIME` (0.25 s) flips him out. The run keeps its speed (v0.34: the v0.33 slowdown to 35% felt like slow motion; `SINK_SLOW` = 1), and the slab ahead keeps crumbling (the hole stretches) so he never sinks into solid floor and the grace is always the full 0.25 s. Otherwise the fall counts as before (life lost and back on the surface, or death). A buffered tap on landing still jumps the hole. The renderer hides his legs only behind the slab in front of the hole.
- Performance (v0.24): `QUALITY` levels in render.js (high / medium / low: canvas resolution cap, draw distance, sea glints, coffers, flutes, speed lines). Opções → "Qualidade" is automatic by default: main.js watches frame times and steps down one level after ~2 s under ~40 fps (`ci-quality-auto`), never back up on its own. Measured in headless Chromium: 60 fps normally; with 6x CPU throttling ~11 fps at high, ~36 fps after stepping down to low.
- Achievements (v0.25, kept basic on purpose): 8 goals in `js/achievements.js` (tutorial, 100/500/1000 m, 300 m without losing a life, 20 flips or 10 slides in a run, 25 runs), stored in `ci-achievements`, checked every 0.5 s during runs and at the end; a toast announces each unlock; menu → "Conquistas" lists them.
